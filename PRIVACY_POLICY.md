# Privacy Policy for X-AutoSlip

**Last Updated:** October 22, 2026

Thank you for choosing **X-AutoSlip** ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application (`com.xautoslip.app`) handles your information when you use our services.

Your privacy is our utmost priority. **X-AutoSlip is designed with a privacy-first, on-device architecture.**

---

### 1. Information Collection and Storage

- **100% On-Device Processing**: All image processing, optical character recognition (OCR), transaction calculations, and categorization take place locally on your mobile device.
- **No Cloud Uploads**: We do **not** transmit, upload, collect, or store your bank transfer slips, transaction details, notes, or balances on any external servers or cloud services.
- **Local Database**: All transactions you record or scan are stored exclusively in your device's local database (Android Room/SQLite). If you uninstall the app or clear app data, this data is permanently removed.

---

### 2. Device Permissions and Usage

X-AutoSlip requests minimal permissions necessary to deliver core features:

- **Photos & Media Access (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`)**:
  - **Purpose**: Used strictly to allow you to select bank transfer slip images from your gallery using Android's photo picker.
  - **Scope**: The application only accesses images that you specifically choose to scan. We do not access, scan, or index any other personal photos or albums.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & Technologies

- **Google ML Kit Text Recognition**:
  - The app uses Google's on-device ML Kit library to extract text (such as transaction amounts and dates) from your slip images.
  - Processing is performed entirely on your device and does not require an active internet connection or external server communication.

---

### 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Because we do not collect your personal data or store it on remote servers:
- We **do not** sell, rent, trade, or share any personal or financial information with third parties.
- We **do not** track your activity across other applications or websites.
- We **do not** display third-party advertisements that collect tracking identifiers.

---

### 5. Security

We are committed to protecting your financial records. Because all information remains within your device's sandboxed local storage, your data benefits directly from Android's built-in operating system security measures and encryption.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy

X-AutoSlip is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

### 7. User Control & Data Deletion

You retain complete ownership and control over your data at all times:
- You can edit or delete any individual transaction inside the app.
- You can delete all application data at any time by going to **Android Settings > Apps > X-AutoSlip > Storage > Clear Data**, or by uninstalling the app.

---

### 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
