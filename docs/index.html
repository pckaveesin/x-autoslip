Privacy Policy

X-AutoSlip: Slip & Expense

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Thank you for choosing X-AutoSlip ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application (com.xautoslip.app) handles your information when you use our services.

Your privacy is our utmost priority. X-AutoSlip is designed with a privacy-first, on-device architecture.

1. Information Collection & Storage

  • 100% On-Device Processing: All image processing, optical character recognition (OCR), transaction calculations, and categorization take place locally on your mobile device.
  • No Cloud Uploads: We do not transmit, upload, collect, or store your bank transfer slips, transaction details, notes, or balances on any external servers or cloud services.
  • Local Database: All transactions you record or scan are stored exclusively in your device's local database (Android Room/SQLite). If you uninstall the app or clear app data, this data is permanently removed.

2. Device Permissions and Usage

X-AutoSlip requests minimal permissions necessary to deliver core features:

  • Photos & Media Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used strictly to allow you to select bank transfer slip images from your gallery using Android's photo picker. The application only accesses images that you specifically choose to scan.

3. Third-Party Services & Technologies

  • Google ML Kit Text Recognition: The app uses Google's on-device ML Kit library to extract text (such as transaction amounts and dates) from your slip images. Processing is performed entirely on your device and does not require an active internet connection or external server communication.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any personal or financial information with third parties.
  • We do not track your activity across other applications or websites.
  • We do not display third-party advertisements that collect tracking identifiers.

5. Security

We are committed to protecting your financial records. Because all information remains within your device's sandboxed local storage, your data benefits directly from Android's built-in operating system security measures and encryption.

6. User Control & Data Deletion

You retain complete ownership and control over your data at all times. You can edit or delete any individual transaction inside the app, or clear all app data anytime via Android Settings.
