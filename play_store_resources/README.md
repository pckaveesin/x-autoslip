# Google Play Store Release Assets Package

All required visual, video, text, and graphic assets for publishing **X-AutoSlip** (`com.xautoslip.app`) to the Google Play Store.

---

## Folder Contents

### 1. `screenshots/` (Phone Screenshots)
* Required by Play Store: Min 2, max 8 screenshots in PNG/JPEG format.
* **`01_dashboard.png`**: Dashboard with monthly cards and transaction history.
* **`02_monthly_summary.png`**: Monthly analytics, category breakdown progress bars.
* **`03_add_transaction.png`**: Income & expense entry form with category tags.
* **`04_slip_scan.png`**: Bank slip scanner interface.

### 2. `graphics/` (Store Graphic Assets)
* **`app_icon_512x512.png`**: 512x512 px 32-bit PNG app icon (Mandatory for Google Play Console).

### 3. `videos/` (Promotional Videos)
* **`xautoslip_merged_16x9.mp4`**: Ready-to-upload 16:9 Landscape (1920x1080 60fps) video with centered phone and blurred ambient backdrop (Ideal for YouTube & Google Play Store listing).
* **`xautoslip_merged_raw.mp4`**: Raw combined vertical mobile capture.
* **`xautoslip_demo_*.mp4`**: Individual screen recording takes.

### 4. `store_listing/` (Copy & Metadata)
* **`STORE_LISTING_EN.md`**: English Title, Short Description, and Full Description (within character limits).
* **`STORE_LISTING_TH.md`**: Thai Title, Short Description, and Full Description (within character limits).
* **`VIDEO_STORYBOARD.md`**: Video script, timing, on-screen captions, and voiceover text for AI tools (CapCut/ElevenLabs).
